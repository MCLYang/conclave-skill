# R3 Convergence — Panelist D (Qwen)

## 立场：反对3个具体条款，同意方向

### 反对条款一：Dynamic Max Rounds（无固定上限）
"无上限"开放了无限辩论可能，但没有配套的防膨胀机制。如果参与者故意持续抛出"新的实质性分歧"，debate 会无限延伸。

**替代文本：**
> Max rounds: dynamic, capped at R8. Dynamic termination evaluation may begin no earlier than after R2 concludes. If the chair determines that rounds R6–R8 show no convergence trajectory (i.e., the count of open strategic/structural items does not decrease across two consecutive rounds), the chair must invoke forced arbitration: the user makes a binding decision, or the item is recorded as an unresolved risk with a rollback plan.

### 反对条款二：R2 Termination 的 Low-Risk 条件过于刻苛
"zero new substantive disagreements" 几乎不可能满足——只要有一个参与者提出任何新异议，R2 就不能终止。这意味着所有话题都至少R3。

**替代文本：**
> For low-risk topics, R2 termination is allowed when the divergence ledger shows zero open strategic/structural items, and any remaining parametric/executional disagreements have been acknowledged (not necessarily resolved) by all panelists as non-blocking. The chair may declare R2 termination if no participant has raised a new substantive disagreement that survives a 5-minute objection window after the chair's provisional close call.

### 反对条款三：Shadow-Mode 验证的数量和范围不足
8-10个样本量太小，尤其要追踪"escape-defect rate"。如果真实逃逸率是10%，10个样本中有30%概率一个都抓不到。

**替代文本：**
> Before full adoption, run a minimum of 20 shadow-mode debates, stratified as follows: at least 8 high-risk topics, at least 8 low-risk topics, and at least 4 mixed-complexity topics. Track escape-defect rate with a confidence interval; adoption requires an upper-bound 95% CI escape rate below 5%.

### 总结
反对的是上述三个具体条款的撧辞，而非整个收敛提案的方向。关于 divergence ledger、终止条件四项要求、以及两轮僵持后的 sub-debate 或风险记录机制——这些我都同意。
